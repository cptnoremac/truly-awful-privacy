Truly Awful — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Truly Awful, an Android comedy app published by cptnoremac, handles information.

Information you enter or create

Truly Awful does not require an account. Its fictional desktop apps may let you enter text or create drawings. That content is used to provide the app's features and is stored locally on your device when you choose to save it. The developer does not operate a server to receive that content.

The app may also remember local preferences and progress.

Fictional advertisements and forms

Some parts of Truly Awful parody advertisements or purchasing forms. These are jokes, not real offers. The fictional forms do not process payments or send entered information to the developer.

Optional purchase through Google Play

If you buy an optional upgrade, payment is handled by Google Play, not by Truly Awful. The app uses Google Play Billing to check purchase status and unlock purchased features. The developer does not receive your payment-card number through the app. Google handles payment-related information under its own Privacy Policy.

Analytics and advertising

Truly Awful does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Its fake advertisements are part of the comedy experience and are not ads served by an advertising network.

Sharing and retention

The developer does not sell or share content you create in Truly Awful with third parties. Content stored locally remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any Android device backup or restore settings you have enabled. Google Play independently processes information needed to provide purchases and related services.

Children

Truly Awful is a general-audience comedy app and is not designed specifically for children. It does not collect personal information from children (or anyone) on a developer-operated server.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, this page will be updated. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or its store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cptnoremac@gmail.com.
